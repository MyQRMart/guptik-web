About

GupTik is the flagship home-server product of MyQRMart. Ideology: data sovereignty — local first, no extra cloud because it is fashionable.

Desktop tabs: Dashboard, Home Control, Settings, GupTik AI, Vault, Media Player, Trust Me, Meta, WhatsApp, Contacts, Data Tables.

Phone is the same product away from the desk. Media Player only lists videos whose node is actually online.

Founder: Koneti Pruthvi Simha Reddy (Simha), Rameshwaram / Proddatur. Parent: StoneAge. Software: MyQRMart. Hardware: Crude Laboratories. No outside investors.
