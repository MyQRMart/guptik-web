Privacy

GupTik is a MyQRMart product (StoneAge). Last updated 17 September 2026.

What we mean

Your vault, Trust Me chats, and media files are stored on the computer you install as the node. We do not run a public video CDN of your library.

Account

Sign-in uses an account email. That identity is how the phone finds the desktop (same email = the pair). Device directory and tunnel hostname exist so the phone can reach your box.

What is not on your node

Meta / WhatsApp Business tokens stay with the account settings used to talk to Meta. Home Control board registry is account-level. We are moving more of this onto the node over time. Bhishma is the intended owner-controlled backend; it is not flipped on yet.

This website

guptik.com is a brochure: install steps, these policies. It does not host your files.
